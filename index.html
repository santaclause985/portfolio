About Me

Turning real-world problems
into working systems

A snapshot of where I'm at, what I do, and what I'm building toward.

I'm a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology graduate with a strong foundation in software development, networking, cybersecurity, and IT support. What drives me isn't just writing code — it's the moment a system moves from spec to something real people depend on every day.

My experience goes beyond the classroom. I've built and deployed business systems, POS solutions, smart automation tools, and networking infrastructure — including CCTV deployments and hands-on technical support — all through actual client engagements and on-site work. I take ownership of projects end-to-end, from initial architecture to production.

Right now, I'm channeling that same build-and-ship mindset toward a career in DevOps Engineering. I'm actively developing skills in CI/CD pipelines, containerization, cloud infrastructure, and deployment automation — because I believe the best software developers aren't just those who write clean code, but those who understand how it lives, scales, and runs in production.

My goal is straightforward: build reliable, modern systems that help small businesses and everyday operations work better — and eventually be the engineer who makes sure those systems never go down.
